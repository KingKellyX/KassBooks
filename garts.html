G Arts Books

Read your General Arts class books on your tablet. Free for KASS students.

Download for Android
Version 1.5Works on Android tablets

What you get

  • All your General Arts class books in one place
  • Read on your tablet, even with no data
  • Updates come to you when new books and/or features are added

How to install

The file is not from the Play Store, so your tablet will ask you to allow it. This is normal.

  1. Download

    Tap the Download button above. Wait for the file to finish.

  2. Open the file

    Open the downloaded APK file. If your tablet asks, tap Settings and allow installs from this source.

  3. Install

    Tap Install, then Open. Your books are ready.
