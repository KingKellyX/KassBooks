How to get your app

It takes about two minutes.

  1. Pick your programme

    Open the Apps page and choose the app for your subject.

  2. Download the file

    Tap the Download button. An APK file will save on your phone.

  3. Install and learn

    Open the file, allow the install, and start learning.
