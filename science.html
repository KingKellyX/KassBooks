Science Books

Read your General Science class books on your tablet. Free for KASS students.

Download for Android
Version 1.0Works on Android tablets

What you get

  • All your Science class books in one place
  • Read on your tablet, even with no data
  • Updates come to you when new books are added

How to install

The file is not from the Play Store, so your phone will ask you to allow it. This is normal.

  1. Download

    Tap the Download button above. Wait for the file to finish.

  2. Open the file

    Open the downloaded APK file. If your phone asks, tap Settings and allow installs from this source.

  3. Install

    Tap Install, then Open. Your books are ready.
